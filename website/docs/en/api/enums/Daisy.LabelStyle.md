# Enumeration: LabelStyle

Describes how to draw a label.

## Enumeration Members

### FILL

> **FILL**: `0`

Fill the text of the label, but do not outline.

***

### FILL\_AND\_OUTLINE

> **FILL\_AND\_OUTLINE**: `2`

Fill and outline the text of the label.

***

### OUTLINE

> **OUTLINE**: `1`

Outline the text of the label, but do not fill.
